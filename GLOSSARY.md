# Mc-incremental

A curated Minecraft modpack built around one mod with slow, steady, long-running progression.

## Language

**Pack**:
The curated set of mods, configs, scripts and quests this repo produces and players install.
_Avoid_: Modpack (fine in prose), instance, profile

**Centrepiece mod**:
The single mod whose progression the whole Pack is built around; every other mod exists to serve it.
_Avoid_: Core mod (means something else in Minecraft modding), main mod

**Support mod**:
Any mod in the Pack that isn't the Centrepiece mod, included either to feed its progression or for quality of life.
_Avoid_: Addon (reserved for mods that extend a specific other mod)

**Progression**:
The ordered path a player takes from a fresh world to the Pack's end goal, gated by effort and complexity rather than waiting.
_Avoid_: Grind, incremental (until incremental mechanics are designed)

**Prestige**:
Deliberately abandoning the current world for a freshly generated one, keeping only what is designed to carry over. The Pack's core loop is resource exhaustion followed by Prestige.
_Avoid_: Reset, rebirth, ascension, new game+

**Progression layer**:
The Pack's own additions on top of its mods (questbook, recipe changes, staged unlocks) that tie them into one Progression.
_Avoid_: Integration, glue
