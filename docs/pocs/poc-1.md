# PoC 1: ProjectE + a tech Content mod

Status: being defined. Decisions here are cheap to change; hard-to-reverse ones live in `docs/adr/`.

## Settled

- **Carry-over:** ProjectE EMC (ADR-0002). A Prestige keeps the world's Knowledge **and** its EMC balance; both are world-global, shared by every player (ADR-0004).
- **Wiped on Prestige:** the inventory, and the world as far as the player is concerned: they move to a fresh Run area and never return to the old one (ADR-0001).
- **Run area:** 3×3 chunks, kept as a tunable setting for play-testing.
- **Confinement:** the vanilla world border, re-centred on each new Run area.
- **Portals do nothing during a Run (#56):** a Run stays in its dimension, which only changes at Prestige (ADR-0008), since the snapshot and the Prestige sweep only cover the Run area. While the world has a Run, lighting a Nether portal frame fails and no portal (Nether, End, End gateway) moves players, mobs or items. Prestige's own teleport still works from any dimension; teleports that aren't portal trips (`/tp`, `/execute in`) are unaffected. See the mod's `docs/run-dimension.md`.
- **Prestige trigger:** a craftable item, cheap enough to make from a single dirt, sand or cobblestone, that runs a KubeJS script: pick a far-away new area (re-pick if ocean), move the border, teleport, clear the inventory.
- **Prestige availability:** always available (no minimum achievement).

- **Content mod and version:** GregTech CEu Modern 8.0 snapshot (pinned) on 1.21.1 NeoForge, with ProjectE PE1.1.0 and KubeJS 2101 (ADR-0006).
- **Ore layout:** GT places one vein per layer on a 3-chunk grid, so each 3×3 Run area holds exactly one stone-layer and one deepslate-layer vein (e.g. iron ~1 in 7 Runs, diamond ~1 in 8.5). That per-Run lottery is the hook. The Prestige script centres each Run area on a GT grid point (chunk x and z both multiples of 3) so the veins sit inside the border. A thin baseline of scattered vanilla ores stays on (`removeVanillaOreGen=false`) so no Run soft-locks the steam age.
- **Allowed low-value inputs:** air, water, cobblestone (GT chemistry needs oxygen; plastics come from plant ethanol).
- **Removed free generation (ADR-0005):** GT Fluid Drilling Rig, Bedrock Ore Miner (`doBedrockOres=false`), the `stone_dust_separation` recipe (cobble to titanium/chrome trickle), ProjectE Energy Collectors and power flowers. Chunk loaders are banned.
- **Every obtainable item and fluid has EMC, except water (#55).** Ore blocks and raw ores are worth what fully processing them yields (see "How EMC values are made"), so mining and banking an ore is as good as processing it, and processing never creates EMC. ProjectE's own ore and raw-material blacklists (which set `c:ores` and `c:raw_materials` to 0) are turned off in `pack/config/ProjectE/mapping.toml`.
- **Prestige sweep:** on Prestige, everything in the Run area that doesn't match the original worldgen (placed blocks, machines and the contents of every machine and storage inventory and tank, placed fluids, dropped items) is converted to EMC at **full value** and removed, so nothing valuable is left behind and nothing keeps generating EMC. Untouched natural blocks (unmined ore, terrain) are left alone and abandoned. The sweep diffs the area against a snapshot taken at Run start (ADR-0007).
- **Custom mod:** the Prestige controller (change tracking, sweep, new Run area, border, teleport, inventory wipe) the EMC barrel (issue #10; outputs only) and the EMC intake (#60; the only automated way into EMC) live together in one repo, `mc-incremental-prestige` (ADR-0003); they can be split later.
- **EMC is lossless and reversible:** converting a resource to EMC and back nets zero (sell value equals EMC value).
- **Fluid EMC is explicit:** each fluid has its own EMC value per 1000 mB in the Pack's EMC data; it is never derived from buckets. Every fluid is listed (#55), so the barrel, the sweep and the GT recipe mapper (#52) all use the same value; only water and fluids nothing in the Pack makes are 0.
- **Power generation:** open question, issue #25.
- **EMC values for GT:** the Prestige mod feeds GT machine recipes into ProjectE (#52), so GT parts and machines are valued from their recipe costs, never from GT's recycling bills. Only what nothing derives is pinned (#55, below).
- **Inventories and ender chests at Prestige:** every ender chest's contents are credited to the world balance and emptied. A player who was offline during a Prestige has their inventory and ender chest credited and cleared when they next join (#49). Every online player's inventory (main, armour, offhand, crafting grid, cursor, and whatever an open menu hands back) is also converted into EMC before the wipe (#57), through the same credit path, so logging out before a Prestige gains nothing. Starting items must be worth 0 EMC (the Pack pins the Transmutation Tablet at 0): they are converted with the rest and handed out again, so a valued starting item would create EMC on every Prestige.
- **ProjectE default EMC values (#47):** wherever ProjectE has a default value it applies (iron ingot 256, copper 128, gold 2048, diamond 8192, crops, mob drops, ...). Since #59, except for GT-mined materials (ProjectE's tin, lead, silver, nickel, osmium, platinum, bronze, ruby and sapphire defaults: priced by rarity like every other GT material), the nether star (its ingredient cost) and the blaze rod (4 blaze powder). Renewables are not devalued; they are judged by the exploit threshold instead: only a loop that lets a player create or duplicate roughly **100+ EMC in an automatable way** gets fixed, and each one is decided individually. Small free-EMC leaks are accepted. ProjectE's Philosopher's Stone conversions (4 gold to diamond, ...) and the Volcanite Amulet are kept: at default values they are EMC-neutral, and the amulet's lava (64 EMC per bucket, after 32 EMC of amulet fuel) is far below the threshold and can't be automated (ProjectE gives it no dispenser behaviour).
- **Where EMC values live (#41, #47, #55):**
  - Item values: `pack/config/ProjectE/custom_emc.json` (ProjectE's own override file, `{"entries":[{"id":"<item>","emc":<long>}]}`; ProjectE applies it after its datapack defaults, so these values win). Fluid values: `pack/kubejs/data/mcincremental_prestige/fluid_emc/fluids.json` (`{"emc_per_1000_mb": {"<source fluid>": <long>}}`), read by the Prestige mod, which also feeds them to its GT recipe mapper; ProjectE can't hold fluid values. **Both files are generated** by `docs/research/emc-pricing/generate.py` (see its `README.md`); edit `hand_values.json` there and re-run it, never the files.
  - Tags are pinned only where #59 overrides ProjectE's tag default (the GT metal and gem tags of tin, lead, silver, nickel, osmium, platinum, bronze, ruby and sapphire; `c:nether_stars`; `c:rods/blaze`), so the others (ingots of iron, gold and copper, gems, the emerald) apply. The vanilla items pinned are ones GT's recipe changes hide from ProjectE (bucket 768, lava bucket 832, iron/gold/copper block = 9 ingots: ProjectE's own values) and obtainable items no recipe makes (below).
  - Filled buckets equal the empty bucket plus the fluid's value, so bucketing a fluid neither gains nor loses EMC. The smoke test checks every listed fluid's bucket.
  - `covalenceLoss = 1.0` is pinned in `pack/config/ProjectE/server.toml`, so sell value equals EMC value.
  - The Transmutation Tablet is pinned at **0 EMC**: the prestige mod gives it to players for free, so burning it or sweeping it on Prestige must yield nothing. The smoke test asserts it.
- **How EMC values are made (#55, re-based in #59 against the Prestige mod v0.3.0).** ProjectE's defaults and the Prestige mod's GT recipe mapper derive almost everything from recipes; since v0.3.0 the mapper splits each GT recipe's cost across all its outputs (the mod's `docs/gt-recipe-emc.md`). `generate.py` replays ProjectE and the mapper's first-output conversions offline (`emcmodel.py`) to decide what to pin, and reads the game back from a dump wherever they differ; the committed values are a fixed point (generating from a dump of a server running them gives them again). Method and files: `docs/research/emc-pricing/README.md`.
  - **The balancing metric is Prestiges per tier: about 5–10 per tier** (owner decision, #59), counting the Runs spent discovering the tier's materials: `Prestiges = discovery Runs + kit cost ÷ net EMC per Run`. `prestiges_per_tier.py` reports it from a dump (committed output: `prestiges_per_tier.txt`): kit = 16 single-block machines of the tier plus its key multiblock (EBF, vacuum freezer, large chemical reactor, assembly line, fusion MK1–3); net EMC per Run = the share of a Run area's ores the previous tier's miner reaches (LV 10%, MV 15%, HV 50%, then all) and 10% of its other blocks, in the best Run dimension the world can reach (Nether from MV, End from EV); discovery = a Monte Carlo game picking among 3 Candidate areas drawn by GT's vein weights. All of these are named assumptions in the script; final balance comes from playtesting. Current result: LV 6.3, MV 8.2, HV 6.8, EV 4.5, IV 5.2, LuV 5.6, ZPM 8.1, UV 9.2, with kit cost rising every tier. LV–EV are set by discovery (kits cost under 1.3M against 6.6–65M per Run); EV sits just under 5.
  - **Leaf materials by rarity** (`rarity.py`). A raw material is priced by how much of it one Run turns up in the dimension that yields the most (a Run lies in one dimension, ADR-0008): ore blocks per Run area (`ore_counts.json`) times what fully processing them yields by the better of the ore washer and chemical bath routes, plus the byproducts of crushing the area's rocks (end stone → tungstate, netherrack → gold, deepslate → thorium). `value = 256 × (f_iron / f) ^ k`, rounded to a power of two, between 16 and 16,384 (the cap is a dial). **k = 1** (owner decision: at least 1, so rare veins pay more per Run and chasing EMC is a real choice between Candidate areas; the Prestiges-per-tier metric is nearly insensitive to it, because a higher k raises both kit costs and EMC per Run, so the lowest allowed value was kept). Iron is the anchor (as common as iron = 256). Rock dusts are never rarity materials: they are worth their rock (tuff dust 4, end stone dust 1).
  - **Units from rarity or ProjectE defaults only, consistent with recipes.** Vanilla materials keep ProjectE's value (iron 256, gold 2,048, diamond 8,192, the nether star's forms); GT-mined metals (tin, lead, silver, nickel, aluminium, platinum, ...) take their rarity price, overriding ProjectE's metal and gem defaults. A unit is lowered to what non-ore recipes make it for (silicon from sand, 12), raised to what smelting or decomposing it gives (goethite → iron; galena → lead + silver), lowered where composing it costs less (massicot from galena), lowered to a cheaper route ProjectE found in the game (`game_caps.json`: U-238, salt, rutile, ...), and alloys are worth their mixer recipe (bronze, stainless steel, ...). Each unit is pinned on the dust, gem and ingot (**dust = ingot**, smoke-tested for every GT metal) and their small forms, and every metal's molten fluid is its ingot per 144 mB, so casting, melting and smelting neither gain nor lose. Ore byproducts are valued by these units only, never by derived dusts, so ore values don't feed back into each other (redstone ore 2,931 instead of 34k, nether saltpeter ore 11,358 instead of 177k).
  - **Rocks and ores at their expected yield.** A rock whose crushing has valuable byproducts is worth its dust plus them (end stone 9, netherrack 17, deepslate 5; tungstate is common once end stone counts, so end stone is far below the ~155 the review estimated). Every ore block, raw ore and ore-processing form (crushed, purified and refined ores, impure and pure dusts) is worth what its best route yields (a value iteration over every recipe that consumes it alone), so mining and banking an ore is as good as processing it (iron ore 3,695, copper ore 4,777: the chemical bath turns copper's byproducts into gold).
  - **Late-tier floors** (owner decision 3, `hand_values.json` `floors`): EV titanium 2,048; IV tungsten 131,072; LuV ruthenium, rhodium, indium and HSS-E 196,608; ZPM europium, trinium and naquadah alloy 32,768; UV americium, darmstadtium, duranium, naquadria, tritanium and neutronium 32,768 (a ZPM/UV floor leaks into the tiers above through shared parts, so they stay low). Only materials no ore yields directly, so floors raise kit costs without raising a Run's ore value; all their forms and their molten fluid follow. Making a floored material gains by design, at its tier; the smoke test exempts those recipes.
  - **Ingredient cost where ProjectE can't map the recipe** (crafting with a GT tool, which only loses durability; GT tools themselves; recipes whose output carries data components): pinned at that recipe's ingredient cost, one step at a time, so everything made from them stays derived; pins the game can derive by itself are dropped.
  - **Below 1 EMC.** ProjectE floors values, so an item whose cheapest recipe costs less than 1 EMC (slabs and buttons cut from 1-EMC stone, tiny dusts) would read 0; those, and items the game floors to 0 although the model values them (`game_zero.json`), are pinned at their value or 1.
  - **Fluids.** Water is 0 and the only free fluid. Lava 64 and milk 16 (ProjectE's bucket values minus the empty bucket), GT fuels hand-set (#41). **Free fluids are priced per tier, anchored at lava's 64** (owner decision, #59): what GT makes from nothing or from water alone (gas collector air LV 64, nether air MV 128, ender air HV 256; the air scrubber's carbon monoxide 64; water electrolysis's hydrogen and oxygen 64) is worth `64 × 2^(tier − 1)` for the cheapest recipe's voltage tier; distilled water and ice stay 1 (water in another form). Everything made from them derives (nitrogen 71, argon 144, ammonia 78, nitric acid 105). An LV gas collector makes 10,000 mB of air per 10 s, so it now banks 64 EMC/s; that is the price of the decision and a playtest question. Every other fluid is listed at the value the mapper derives for it, then lowered where listing it made a recipe cheaper.
  - **Hand pins:** steel 288 and wrought iron 256 (the primitive blast furnace makes steel from either and nearly free coke or charcoal; v0.3.0 would otherwise lower both through gaining decompositions of floored tool heads); blaze rod 576 (4 blaze powder, which GT synthesises from carbon and sulfur; ProjectE's 1,536 gained ~1,000 per rod). **Nether star 24,772** (owner decision 5): the wither skeleton skull is priced by rarity at 8,192 and the star at its ingredients, 3 skulls + 4 soul sand, so the automatable Wither loop gains nothing.
  - **Obtainable items no recipe makes** (owner decisions, `hand_values.json`): farmable mob drops and loot kept under 100 (totem of undying, trial keys, experience bottle 64), goat horn 192, elytra 8,192, dragon head 4,096, amethyst cluster 128 and buds 32, mushroom blocks 32, bee nest 45, gilded blackstone 908 (4 gold nuggets), axolotl/tadpole bucket 832/800, GT marble and red granite 16 (like vanilla granite), GT concrete and construction foam 1, brittle charcoal 16.
  - **Still 0, allow-listed in the smoke test:** water; creative-only and technical items (spawn eggs, command blocks, GT creative items); blocks no survival player can pick up (bedrock, spawners, budding amethyst, infested blocks, ...); potions, tipped arrows and the ominous bottle (ProjectE values them per contents); GT items with no recipe (energy converters, solar panels, passthrough hatches, casings above UHV, ...); the removed Fluid Drilling Rig, Bedrock Ore Miner and Energy Collectors; fluids nothing makes (bromine, oil and light oil, UU matter, ...).
- **Emerald (#53 undone by #55):** the emerald has ProjectE's default 16,384 again and the Philosopher's Stone `emerald_to_diamond`/`diamond_to_emerald` conversions are back, so equivalence holds. Trades are intentionally a bit unbalanced; villager trades that buy an emerald with cheap items now gain up to its value (the #53 list of trades stands).
- **The exploit check (#59: ratio-based).** `scripts/smoke-test.sh` fails on any ProjectE "EMC Exploit" warning gaining 100 or more, except recipes making a floored late-tier material and outputs `hand_values.json` lists under `accepted_exploits` with a reason (diesel blending, fusing iron plasma). ProjectE only sees what it maps: `docs/research/emc-pricing/audit.py` replays every GT machine recipe against the in-game values and lists those whose outputs are worth more than **1.1× their inputs** while one machine gains at least 5 EMC per second at the recipe's own speed (committed output: `audit.txt`). It lists apart the free resources (no consumed input: rock breaker, gas collectors), the floored materials (intended), and rounding (under 10 EMC per run), and checks invariants (dust = ingot, free fluids above 1). What remains is listed there and summarised in the #59 report: cheap or renewable inputs decomposed into rarity-priced elements (rock dusts, clay, ash, potash, biotite), organic chemistry splitting off free gases at their tier price, multi-step ore chains whose intermediates the mapper splits below their rarity-priced products (platinum group, naquadah, rare earth, bauxite), and refinery fuels against the hand-set GT fuels (#41).

## Owner decisions (code review of #24)

The review flagged these as possible mismatches; the owner kept them as they are:

- **EMC values stay in two files:** items in `pack/config/ProjectE/custom_emc.json`, fluids in `pack/kubejs/data/mcincremental_prestige/fluid_emc/fluids.json`, because ProjectE can't hold fluid values.
- **Server `simulation-distance` stays 2:** the smallest value that keeps the whole 3×3 Run area ticking from a corner chunk. Singleplayer matches it via the Prestige mod's cap (#50); a larger configured Run area raises that cap automatically, but dedicated-server owners must raise `simulation-distance` themselves.
- **The vanilla pins stay:** `minecraft:bucket`, `minecraft:lava_bucket` and the iron/gold/copper blocks are pinned in `custom_emc.json`. They equal ProjectE's defaults; GT's recipes would otherwise leave them at 0.
